OUR JOURNEY & MILESTONES

Key Milestones & School History

A 20+ year legacy of academic excellence, student achievements, and community values in rural Rajasthan.

2025–26

Modern Learning & Future Growth

The school continues its mission of shaping bright futures by integrating modern teaching aids, digital student services, and comprehensive exam preparations.

2020

Excellence in Board Examination Results

Students achieved outstanding academic marks in Class 10th RBSE board exams, bringing immense pride to Mungthala and Sirohi district.

2018

New Campus Building Expansion

Moved into an upgraded campus building with spacious ventilated classrooms, dedicated playground space, and enhanced facilities.

2016

Historic Navodaya Selection Milestone

Achieved a landmark record when 10 students were selected simultaneously for Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalaya, showcasing the academic quality of the school.

2004

School Establishment

Madhusudan Bal Vidya Mandir was established in Mungthala village to bring accessible, value-based quality education to rural students.

Madhusudan Bal Vidya Mandir
Mungthala, Abu Road

Established in 2004, MBVM is a Hindi-medium school offering quality education from Nursery to Class 10 in the rural area of Abu Road, Sirohi District, Rajasthan. We are committed to nurturing every child's potential with values, discipline, and academic excellence.
