Class Three Overview

Class Three focuses on strengthening students' reading comprehension, basic mathematics, environment studies, and Hindi grammar. Through interactive classroom discussions, visual aids, and regular practice assignments, students develop a love for learning and disciplined daily study habits.

Key Highlights

  • Personalized attention from dedicated class teachers.
  • Continuous monthly evaluation and parent-teacher interaction.
  • Emphasis on neat handwriting, reading aloud, and mental math skills.
  • Balanced inclusion of physical activities, moral science, and art.

Subjects & Syllabus

  • Hindi (हिन्दी): Reader, Grammar & Creative Writing
  • Mathematics (गणित): Numbers, Operations & Basic Geometry
  • Environmental Studies (पर्यावरण अध्ययन): Nature, Family & Community
  • English (अंग्रेजी): Vocabulary & Sentence Building
  • Moral Education & Games: Values, Discipline & Physical Health
