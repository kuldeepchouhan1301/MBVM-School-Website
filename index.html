ABOUT OUR SCHOOL

Growing Together, Learning for Life

Dedicated to quality education in rural Rajasthan since 2004 — nurturing character, curiosity, and confidence in every student.

At MBVM, we believe education is about more than textbooks. We create a supportive environment where students discover their potential, develop strong values, and prepare confidently for competitive success.

Rural area of Abu Road Block, Sirohi District, Rajasthan (Mungthala).

Affordable and quality education for a brighter future.

50+ students admitted to JNV (Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalaya) — highest in the entire district by 2025–26.

Academics, discipline, moral values, personality development, and competitive preparation.
