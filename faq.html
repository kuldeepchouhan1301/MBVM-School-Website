FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Common Parent & Student Queries

Find clear answers regarding admissions, academics, school timings, transport, and campus policies at MBVM School.

The school provides quality Hindi-medium education from Nursery / LKG to Class 10th. We focus on building a strong academic foundation along with discipline and overall personality development of students.

Madhusudan Bal Vidya Mandir Mungthala was established in 2004. Since then, the school has been committed to providing quality education and moral values to students in the rural area of Abu Road.

Yes, the school is a co-educational institution where both boys and girls study together in a supportive, safe, and disciplined learning environment.

The primary medium of instruction in the school is Hindi. Teachers use simple, interactive, and effective teaching methods for better understanding by every student.

Parents can apply for admission by visiting the school campus office in Mungthala or by filling out the online admission application form on this website.

Documents such as birth certificate, previous class marksheet / transfer certificate (TC), passport-size photographs, and Aadhaar card / ID proof are required during admission.

Transport guidance and arrangements are available depending on route availability. Parents can contact the school admin office for detailed route information.

School timings follow the standard Rajasthan academic calendar (morning shift). Punctuality and regular attendance are strictly encouraged.

Yes, the school encourages students to participate in extracurricular activities such as dance, music, sports, speech competitions, and cultural celebrations.

The school strongly emphasizes moral science, respect, punctuality, and clean habits. Dedicated teachers guide students to develop self-discipline and positive character.
